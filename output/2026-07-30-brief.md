## Frontier / Foundation Models (22)

### California

**SB 1106** -- Agentic artificial intelligence.  
This bill adds "agentic AI" — systems that can take actions on their own rather than just generate content — to two existing state AI oversight requirements: the Office of Emergency Services' risk analysis of AI threats to critical infrastructure must now address it in its recommendations, and the Department of Technology's annual inventory of high-risk automated systems used by state agencies must now catalog agentic AI too. Practically, it means state agencies will have to identify and report any agentic AI tools they're using, building, or buying.

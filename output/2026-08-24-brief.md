**AB 2392** -- Public postsecondary education: generative artificial intelligence systems: procurement standards: training.  
California's community colleges and CSU (with UC asked to join voluntarily) would have to form a joint working group that delivers recommended standards for buying AI tools and for AI training by January 1, 2028, then actually deliver that training to students, faculty, and staff, refresh it yearly, and track who completed it.
